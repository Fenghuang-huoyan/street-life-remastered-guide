## What the list tells you

- Every robbery badge is for a big target: the bank, the jewelry store, the museum and the yacht. See [Robberies](/robberies/street-life-remastered-robberies).
- Property badges cover the penthouse and the mansion, and there are first-car and first-boat badges. Buying property is a real progression goal.
- "Won a Rare Car!" is earned by spinning the wheel, which links to [Cars](/cars/street-life-remastered-cars).
- "You got 1M!" is awarded for having over 1 million in your bank.
