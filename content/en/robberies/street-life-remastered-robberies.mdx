## Robberies confirmed by the game itself

The game awards a badge for each of these, so they are official targets. The badge names come from Roblox's badge list for the game.

- **The bank.** Badge: "You robbed the bank!"
- **The jewelry store.** Badge: "You Robbed the Jewelry Store!"
- **The museum.** Badge: "You Robbed the Museum!"
- **The yacht.** Badge: "You Robbed The Yacht!"
- **Airdrops.** Badge: "You Opened a Airdrop!" (the spelling is the game's own)

Full badge list on [Badges](/achievements/street-life-remastered-badges). The official description also lists robbing banks and houses. Dollar amounts are not in the badge text. The figures further down are from creator videos, and the games API reported an experience update on October 15, 2026, after all of those videos.

Payouts, printers, and the early-cash order are on [Money Methods](/money/street-life-remastered-money-methods).

## What to bring

| Supply | Who needed it |
| --- | --- |
| A gun | Stores and gas stations. Mix, August 2026, pulls a gun and the clerk hands over the cash. Other players also show up, so the gun is for the fight as well as the clerk. |
| Lockpicks | Houses. Both Mix (August 2026) and Trizzleyy (April 2026) buy them before the door. |
| A pickup truck | Houses. Mix named a TRX or a Silverado so the truck can carry furniture. See [Cars](/cars/street-life-remastered-cars). |
| C4 | Bank, and the museum. Mix buys it from the merchant. |
| Duffel bag | Yacht, and Mix also bought them before jewelry. Trizzleyy bought one duffel for the bank and the yacht together. |
| A mask | Museum only, in what Mix stated: two masks, two C4, and two players. He did not say where the mask is bought. |
| Other players | Museum, as a requirement Mix stated. The bank and jewelry store were done with a group in his videos. He did not say a second player is required for stores, houses, or the yacht. |

The merchant is where Mix buys C4, duffel bags, credit cards, and lockpicks.

## Stores and gas stations

**1.** Bring a gun.

**2.** Aim at the clerk and take the cash.

**3.** Spots Mix actually robbed on August 16, 2026: the supermarket connected to the dealership, the gas station, and two stores next to the chop shop. A corner store on that first street could not be robbed when he tried.

**4.** Expect other players. In that video, people pulled up while he was inside a store.

He also came back to stores later and found one gas station not ready. He put the museum, jewelry store, and bank on a cooldown of about 10 to 30 minutes. He did not give stores their own cooldown number. **Unconfirmed** how long a store or gas station stays empty.

Amounts from that run are in the payout table below.

## Houses

**1.** Buy lockpicks from the merchant.

**2.** Get a pickup. Mix used a TRX or a Silverado. Trizzleyy used a Silverado.

**3.** Lockpick the door. Trizzleyy described the door minigame as something you have to get right. He did not list the controls.

**4.** Carry furniture out to the truck. Mix took a fridge, a microwave, a couch, and a table from one place, then a TV and a safe from a second house. Trizzleyy took household items including a TV.

**5.** Sell at the warehouse. Mix put the warehouse next to the kick store and gun shop number two. At the second house he sold to a buyer standing next to the building.

Called-out prices and the $100,000 cash moment are on [Money Methods](/money/street-life-remastered-money-methods). The $100,000 includes earlier store robberies, not just the furniture.

## Bank

Two videos show the bank, and they do not show the same detail.

Mix, June 7, 2026, shows gang members blowing the bank open with C4. That video is about joining a gang, not a full bank walkthrough, and it does not state a payout.

Trizzleyy, April 25, 2026, shows these steps:

**1.** Buy C4 at the merchant. He also bought a duffel bag in the same stop because he planned to do the yacht next.

**2.** Go inside and plant the C4. He said the plant works like the lockpick: get all three lines right, and the first line starts out slow.

**3.** Grab money or gold. He said he did not know which pays more, and that either one still pays.

**4.** He then went AFK and missed the grab on camera. Afterward he said the robbery paid $11,000.

He did that run with a bodyguard and no gun. He did not explain how the bodyguard was hired. Mix's August video is the one that says to expect a fight at big robberies. Bring a gun anyway.

**Unconfirmed:** the current bank payout. $11,000 is one April result, partly off camera. Mix did not show a bank payout in August.

## Yacht

**1.** Bring a duffel bag. Both creators treated it as required to carry the loot. Mix, already on the yacht, said he did not have one.

**2.** Take what is still there. Mix came away with a diamond watch and said another player had left loot behind. Trizzleyy arrived while the yacht was on cooldown and the loot was already gone, so he showed no yacht payout.

**3.** Sell at the loot buyer. Mix sold the diamond watch for about $25,000 in the same stop as refined oil.

Mix's 10 to 30 minute cooldown line names the museum, the jewelry store, and the bank. It does not name the yacht. Trizzleyy found the yacht on cooldown and did not time it. **Unconfirmed** yacht cooldown.

## Museum

Mix, August 16, 2026, said a museum robbery needs two players, two masks, and two C4. He was alone, so he could not start one.

What he did instead: other players had already started it. He parked at the door, went in, and the robbery turned into a fight. He told a nearby player to take a gun. He did not show a museum payout, and he did not show where to buy the masks.

## Jewelry store

Mix did not show how a jewelry robbery is started. He arrived while other players were already inside, told his group to buy duffel bags and C4, and took jewelry they handed him. He then sold that jewelry. He never stated a price for a single piece. Do not read the $1.8 million cash callout from that part of the video as a jewelry payout. It is his total cash.

## Airdrops

The badge "You Opened a Airdrop!" confirms that opening one is a real action. Neither money video showed where an airdrop lands, what it contains, or what it pays. **Unconfirmed** beyond the badge.

## Cooldowns and competition

Mix says the museum, jewelry store, and bank have a cooldown of about 10 to 30 minutes. That wait is the reason his August run took as long as it did. Other players contest the same targets: they pulled up on his store robbery, and the museum he joined was already a fight.

He did not give a separate cooldown for stores, houses, the yacht, or airdrops.

## Unconfirmed

- Any payout after the experience update timestamp of October 15, 2026.
- The bank payout after April 25, 2026.
- Museum and jewelry payouts. Neither video showed one.
- Precise cooldown per target. Only the shared 10 to 30 minute range was stated, and only for the museum, jewelry store, and bank.
- Where museum masks are bought.
- Where airdrops spawn and what they pay.
- Whether the bank or the jewelry store can be started solo. Both were shown with other people. Only the museum had an explicit two-player requirement.
