## Robberies confirmed by the game itself

The game awards a badge for each of these, so they are official targets. The badge names come from Roblox's badge list for the game.

- **The bank.** Badge: "You robbed the bank!"
- **The jewelry store.** Badge: "You Robbed the Jewelry Store!"
- **The museum.** Badge: "You Robbed the Museum!"
- **The yacht.** Badge: "You Robbed The Yacht!"
- **Airdrops.** Badge: "You Opened a Airdrop!" (the spelling is the game's own)

Full badge list on [Badges](/achievements/street-life-remastered-badges). The official description also lists robbing banks and houses. Dollar amounts are not in the badge text. The figures further down are from creator videos. On 2026-11-18 the games API reported an experience update at 2026-11-17T01:23:21.08Z, after every video cited on this page. Those dollar figures were not re-measured after that timestamp.

Payouts, printers, and the early-cash order are on [Money Methods](/money/street-life-remastered-money-methods).

## What to bring

| Supply | Who needed it |
| --- | --- |
| A gun | Stores and gas stations. Mix, August 2026, pulls a gun and the clerk hands over the cash. Other players also show up, so the gun is for the fight as well as the clerk. |
| Lockpicks | Houses. Both Mix (August 2026) and Trizzleyy (April 2026) buy them before the door. |
| A pickup truck | Houses. Mix named a TRX or a Silverado so the truck can carry furniture. See [Cars](/cars/street-life-remastered-cars). |
| C4 | Bank, and the museum. Mix buys it from the merchant. |
| Duffel bag | Yacht, and Mix also bought them before jewelry. Trizzleyy bought one duffel for the bank and the yacht together. |
| A mask | Museum only, in what Mix stated: two masks, two C4, and two players. He did not say where the mask is bought. |
| Other players | Museum, as a requirement Mix stated. The bank and jewelry store were done with a group in his videos. He did not say a second player is required for stores, houses, or the yacht. |

The merchant is where Mix buys C4, duffel bags, credit cards, and lockpicks.

## Stores and gas stations

**1.** Bring a gun.

**2.** Aim at the clerk and take the cash.

**3.** Spots Mix actually robbed on August 16, 2026: the supermarket connected to the dealership, the gas station, and two stores next to the chop shop. A corner store on that first street could not be robbed when he tried.

**4.** Expect other players. In that video, people pulled up while he was inside a store.

He also came back to stores later and found one gas station not ready. He put the museum, jewelry store, and bank on a cooldown of about 10 to 30 minutes. He did not give stores their own cooldown number. **Unconfirmed** how long a store or gas station stays empty.

Amounts from that run are in the payout table below.

## Houses

**1.** Buy lockpicks from the merchant.

**2.** Get a pickup. Mix used a TRX or a Silverado. Trizzleyy used a Silverado.

**3.** Lockpick the door. Trizzleyy described the door minigame as something you have to get right. He did not list the controls.

**4.** Carry furniture out to the truck. Mix took a fridge, a microwave, a couch, and a table from one place, then a TV and a safe from a second house. Trizzleyy took household items including a TV.

**5.** Sell at the warehouse. Mix put the warehouse next to the kick store and gun shop number two. At the second house he sold to a buyer standing next to the building.

Called-out prices and the $100,000 cash moment are on [Money Methods](/money/street-life-remastered-money-methods). The $100,000 includes earlier store robberies, not just the furniture.

## Bank

Two videos show the bank, and they do not show the same detail.

Mix, June 7, 2026, shows gang members blowing the bank open with C4. That video is about joining a gang, not a full bank walkthrough, and it does not state a payout.

Trizzleyy, April 25, 2026, shows these steps:

**1.** Buy C4 at the merchant. He also bought a duffel bag in the same stop because he planned to do the yacht next.

**2.** Go inside and plant the C4. He said the plant works like the lockpick: get all three lines right, and the first line starts out slow.

**3.** Grab money or gold. He said he did not know which pays more, and that either one still pays.

**4.** He then went AFK and missed the grab on camera. Afterward he said the robbery paid $11,000.

He did that run with a bodyguard and no gun. He did not explain how the bodyguard was hired. Mix's August video is the one that says to expect a fight at big robberies. Bring a gun anyway.

**Unconfirmed:** the current bank payout. $11,000 is one April result, partly off camera. Mix did not show a bank payout in August.

## Yacht

**1.** Bring a duffel bag. Both creators treated it as required to carry the loot. Mix, already on the yacht, said he did not have one.

**2.** Take what is still there. Mix came away with a diamond watch and said another player had left loot behind. Trizzleyy arrived while the yacht was on cooldown and the loot was already gone, so he showed no yacht payout.

**3.** Sell at the loot buyer. Mix sold the diamond watch for about $25,000 in the same stop as refined oil.

Mix's 10 to 30 minute cooldown line names the museum, the jewelry store, and the bank. It does not name the yacht. Trizzleyy found the yacht on cooldown and did not time it in the April video. A July 17, 2025 yacht video states 30 minutes; that reading is in the November 18 recheck below and was not repeated after the November 17, 2026 update. **Unconfirmed** as the live yacht cooldown.

## Museum

Mix, August 16, 2026, said a museum robbery needs two players, two masks, and two C4. He was alone, so he could not start one.

What he did instead: other players had already started it. He parked at the door, went in, and the robbery turned into a fight. He told a nearby player to take a gun. He did not show a museum payout, and he did not show where to buy the masks.

## Jewelry store

Mix did not show how a jewelry robbery is started. He arrived while other players were already inside, told his group to buy duffel bags and C4, and took jewelry they handed him. He then sold that jewelry. He never stated a price for a single piece. Do not read the $1.8 million cash callout from that part of the video as a jewelry payout. It is his total cash.

## Airdrops

The badge "You Opened a Airdrop!" confirms that opening one is a real action. Neither money video showed where an airdrop lands, what it contains, or what it pays. **Unconfirmed** beyond the badge.

## Cooldowns and competition

Mix says the museum, jewelry store, and bank have a cooldown of about 10 to 30 minutes. That wait is the reason his August run took as long as it did. Other players contest the same targets: they pulled up on his store robbery, and the museum he joined was already a fight.

He did not give a separate cooldown for stores, houses, the yacht, or airdrops in that August video. Older on-screen timers are in the recheck below. None of them was read again after the November 17, 2026 update.

### Jewelry store timer and sales

Mix, "How to ROB the JEWELRY STORE in STREET LIFE REMASTERED (Roblox)" (YouTube, 2025-06-14):

1. Buy a duffel bag from the illegal dealer. He placed that dealer around the corner from TNT and paid $500 in-game for one bag. The Scammer pass, still listed on 2026-11-18, says the illegal dealer is near the Ice Cream Job. Whether that is the same spot as his TNT landmark is **Unconfirmed**.
2. Pull a gun to start it. The game offered a break-glass prompt. He shot the glass and said that was faster.
3. Without a game pass he could carry two pieces. He said the 2x duffel-size pass was 450 Robux and let him carry more, including a chain the default bag would not take. The catalog read on 2026-11-18 prices that pass at 500 Robux and limits the description to the bank and the jewelry store. He also mentioned a double-illegal-cash pass and did not state its price. The same catalog prices "2X ILLEGAL CASH" at 850 Robux.
4. First sale: he called out $12,000, said he usually gets about $18,000, then later called that same sale $13,000. Second sale: $19,000. He summed the two runs as about $30,000. The first sale is not one exact number.
5. Reset timer on screen: 760 seconds. He called that about 10 to 12 minutes, waited about that long, and the store was ready when he returned. His August 16, 2026 video only gave the museum, jewelry store, and bank a shared "about 10 to 30 minutes" and did not repeat 760 seconds.
6. He said a car despawns in 90 seconds, which is why he left quickly. Auto-captions did not settle item names ("plain Jane", "minus watch", "plane chain", "Cuban chain"). Those spellings are not official names.

### Yacht timer and what sold

Mix, "How to ROB the YACHT in STREET LIFE REMASTERED (Roblox)" (YouTube, 2025-07-17):

1. Duffel bags come from the illegal merchant and are required to start the heist.
2. He said there is a 30 minute timer, and you wait 30 minutes before robbing the yacht again. The August 16, 2026 video did not repeat that number.
3. Jet ski from the pier: $250,000. He could not withdraw $250,000 at once. The ATM cap he hit was $200,000 per pull. He said you can swim there if you cannot buy the jet ski. Green parking spots keep a car; spawning the jet ski despawned his car anyway.
4. About five armored NPCs ("mercenaries" / "juggernauts"). He said headshots were the fast way to drop them. The interior door opened after they were down.
5. Crates gave him a P90, gold, a purple gun (captions: "purple button"), and a diamond watch. He sold the diamond watch for $25,000, the same figure as the August 16 sale. He sold gold in the same stop, said "$122,000," and corrected himself. He then called that stop "a quick $40,000" while he still had the purple gun and the P90. Separate gold price: **Unconfirmed**. He said the purple gun could sell for $50,000. That sale is not in the transcript. **Unconfirmed**.
6. He said a 2x duffel pass, 450 Robux in this July 2025 video, would hold more yacht loot. The pass description read on 2026-11-18 names the bank and the jewelry store, not the yacht, and the price on that read was 500 Robux. Whether the pass doubles yacht loot is **Unconfirmed**. He was not sure the robbery notifies other players. That notification is **Unconfirmed**.

### Museum loot, and two rates that do not match

Mix, "NEW SUMMER UPDATE in STREET LIFE REMASTERED (Roblox)" (YouTube, 2026-06-16), after the museum badge existed:

- Sniper: only from the museum. He said the chance is 0.1%, and "not even 1%."
- AP skeleton watch: only from the museum. He said 5%, and that you cannot buy it.
- Black G-Shock: he said it also comes from the museum, and "I'm pretty sure you can just buy it." **Unconfirmed**.
- In the vault he named lasers to dodge, an exit puzzle, a dinosaur, diamonds, a gold chain, gold bars, ivory, and a gold Roblox trophy. He said a random box can give the sniper or the AP skeleton. He said that attempt took about 30 minutes because of the fight. That is how long the run took, not a cooldown he read off a timer.
- No cash sale of museum loot in that video.

Trizzleyy, "Not ending the video until I get the 0.1% Museum Sniper in: Street life Remastered" (YouTube, 2026-06-23):

- The title says 0.1%. On camera he said he was going for the "1% sniper" and that the box "literally says random." He did not read a percent off the UI. Mix's 0.1% and this spoken "1%" are not the same claim. Current sniper rate: **Unconfirmed**.
- He got the mask at a mask shop. The caption gives no street or shop name beyond that. Exact location: **Unconfirmed**. The August page note that Mix never showed the mask seller still stands for Mix's video.
- The museum was on lockdown. He read 9 minutes until it opened. At the end he said the cooldown is about 18 minutes ("wait 18 minutes all over again"). Both numbers are his words in that video. They sit inside Mix's later "about 10 to 30 minutes" and were not re-read after 2026-11-17.
- The random box gave a Midas watch, not the sniper. He did not sell it. What a Midas watch pays: **Unconfirmed**.
- He still needed C4. One plant failed because they did not have it.
