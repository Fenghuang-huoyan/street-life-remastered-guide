## Robberies confirmed by the game itself

The game awards a badge for each of these, so they are official targets, not just creator lore. The badge names come from Roblox's badge list for the game.

- **The bank.** Badge: "You robbed the bank!"
- **The jewelry store.** Badge: "You Robbed the Jewelry Store!"
- **The museum.** Badge: "You Robbed the Museum!"
- **The yacht.** Badge: "You Robbed The Yacht!"
- **Airdrops.** Badge: "You Opened a Airdrop!" (the spelling is the game's own)

Full badge list on [Badges](/achievements/street-life-remastered-badges). The official description also lists robbing banks and houses.

## Requirements creators showed

| Target | What creators showed |
| --- | --- |
| Museum | Two players, two masks and two C4 (Mix, August 2026) |
| Bank | Blown open with C4 by a group of gang members (Mix, June 2026) |
| Jewelry store | Robbed by a group and the jewelry sold to the loot buyer (Mix, August 2026) |
| Yacht | Loot carried in a duffel bag; loot included a diamond watch (Mix, August 2026) |
| Stores and gas stations | A gun is enough; the clerk hands over the cash (Mix, August 2026) |
| Houses | Lockpick the door, carry items out in a pickup truck, sell at the warehouse (Mix, August 2026) |

Supplies such as C4, duffel bags and lockpicks come from the merchant, per the same video.

## Cooldowns and competition

Mix says the museum, jewelry store and bank have a cooldown of about 10 to 30 minutes. Other players also show up at big robberies: in his video, people arrive while he is robbing, and he stops a museum robbery in progress. Bring a gun and expect a fight.

## Unconfirmed

- The exact payout of the bank and the museum. The August 2026 video did not show a full bank robbery payout.
- Precise cooldown per target. Only the shared 10 to 30 minute range was stated.
