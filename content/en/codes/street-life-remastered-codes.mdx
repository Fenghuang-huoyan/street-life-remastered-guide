## Executable steps: free rewards that do exist

### 1) Claim group rewards

1. Join the **game’s official Roblox community** (linked from the experience page).
2. In-game, use the group-reward claim flow Mix showed (August 16, 2026).
3. In that video the claim paid about **$12,000** and a **Scorpion** gun. Treat amounts as dated.

These are **not** redeem codes. Details: [Money methods](/money/street-life-remastered-money-methods).

### 2) Playtime rewards and the wheel spin

1. Stay in-session long enough for playtime milestones (exact timers not re-measured here).
2. Use the **wheel spin** when the UI offers it—Mix named both as cash sources in the same August 2026 run.
3. Log what you actually receive; do not copy outdated payout screenshots from random sites.

### 3) Airdrops

1. Watch for airdrop events on the map.
2. Open the drop when you can contest it.
3. There is an official badge for opening an airdrop—proof the feature is real even when loot varies.

### 4) If you still want to “try a code”

1. Only use a string posted with a **date** by the developer or a tracker that shows last-checked time.
2. Find the redeem UI in *that* announcement’s instructions—this guide will not invent a menu path.
3. If redeem fails, assume expired or fake; return to group / playtime / wheel / money methods.
