## Short answer

As of September 19, 2026 we have not found any redeemable code for Street Life Remastered. The official description on Roblox does not mention a redeem box or any code, and the 13 badges are all gameplay achievements (see [Badges](/achievements/street-life-remastered-badges)). An independent tracker, Earnaldo, reached the same conclusion, verified on September 3, 2026 and updated September 14, 2026.

## Why "codes" show up online anyway

Earnaldo says the number strings shared as Street Life codes are Roblox asset IDs, such as crosshair or music IDs, not rewards you can redeem. They may be useful for customization, but they do not give cash or items.

## Free rewards that do exist

- **Group rewards.** Claim them from the game's group. In Mix's August 2026 video they paid about $12,000 and a Scorpion gun.
- **Playtime rewards and the wheel spin.** Both were mentioned in the same video as ways to get cash.
- **Airdrops.** There is an official badge for opening one.

See [Money Methods](/money/street-life-remastered-money-methods).

## Unconfirmed

- Whether the developer will add codes in future. This page changes only when an official source or an actively updated tracker confirms a working code.
