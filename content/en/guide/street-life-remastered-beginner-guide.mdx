## What the game is

Street Life Remastered is a free Roblox open-world role-playing game by flipsy's games, released on October 27, 2024. The developer's own description lists what you can do:

- Rob banks and houses, cause chaos and explode cars
- Join the police force and fight crime
- Join a faction and take over turfs for prizes
- Hunt for weapons and cash
- Start a music career at the studio and train at the gym
- Buy your own apartment and flex exotic cars
- Trade crypto on your phone and share your journey on the in-game social media

Servers hold up to 35 players. Owners of private servers get admin inside them (see [Admin](/admin/street-life-remastered-admin-commands)).

On October 15, 2026 the Roblox games API reported an experience update that same day. The cash figures in this guide come from videos recorded before that timestamp.

## The three things to sort out early

A March 2025 beginner video by DopegeekG boils the start of the game down to three things: **guns, a gang and money**. Guns come from two places: turfs, where gang crates hold a wide range of weapons for free, and gun stores, which the creator called a poor early option because guns cost a lot there. Joining a gang is how you get access to a turf, so he recommends doing it as early as possible. See [Gangs](/gangs/street-life-remastered-gangs) and [Guns](/guns/street-life-remastered-guns).

You need a gun before the first store robbery. Mix's August 2026 money run also got a Scorpion from the group rewards, which is a second early gun that does not require the gun store.

## Your first cash

Two creator videos, from March 2025 and August 2026, agree on the starting pattern: do the cheap things first. The steps below follow Mix's August 16, 2026 video, which starts at $0 cash. Item prices, printer timers, and the later methods are on [Money Methods](/money/street-life-remastered-money-methods). How each robbery starts is on [Robberies](/robberies/street-life-remastered-robberies).

**1. Claim the group rewards.** Mix got about $12,000 and a Scorpion. This is not a redeem code. [Codes](/codes/street-life-remastered-codes) explains why the number strings posted online do not pay cash.

**2. Rob a store, then the gas station.** Pull a gun on the clerk. In the August run the supermarket by the dealership paid about $7,000 and the gas station about $11,000. A corner store could not be robbed at that moment. Two stores by the chop shop then paid about $15,000 each. He put those small robberies together at about $60,000. Other players may drive up while you are inside.

**3. Buy printers with that cash, and hide them.** Mix did this before houses. The basic printer in that video costs $2,000 and returns $4,000. Premium printers, as he stated them, pay about $15,000 every 5 minutes. He placed his behind the chop shop. An April 2026 video describes different printer timers from an apartment computer. Those two descriptions do not match, so the current timer is **Unconfirmed**. Collect whatever you placed, then keep moving.

**4. Buy lockpicks at the merchant, then loot a house with a pickup.** Mix named a TRX or a Silverado. See [Cars](/cars/street-life-remastered-cars). One sale in that run was about $5,000, a fridge about $7,000, and a safe about $15,000.

After that, his video moves to the yacht, oil, and the big robberies, with waits of about 10 to 30 minutes on the museum, jewelry store, and bank. That is past the first burst of cash. Use the money page for those steps rather than guessing amounts.

## Older starting options (March 2025)

DopegeekG's beginner video also showed two actions Mix did not price in 2026:

- **Area claims.** Claiming an area paid about $5,000 to $10,000, and it took a while. Mix said in August 2026 that taking a zone to 100% still pays cash, but he did not say how much.
- **Parked cars.** Breaking the passenger window paid about $1,000 in the 2025 video. Mix robbed a car in the 2026 video and did not say what it paid.

A store in the 2025 video paid about $2,000. That is lower than the stores Mix priced in 2026. Amounts differ by shop and by update.

## What changed since 2025

The beginner video is from March 2025, and the game has updated many times since then. Mix says in August 2026 that recent seasons and updates added money methods and rebalanced others. The games API then reported a further experience update on October 15, 2026. Treat any specific gang, price, or payout from an older video as a starting point, and check it in game.

## Unconfirmed

- Exact payouts for every activity. They vary per run, creators show different numbers, and none of these videos is newer than the October 15, 2026 update timestamp.
- Whether the gang names recommended in older videos are still the best beginner picks.
- Which printer timer is current. The August 2026 and April 2026 videos do not agree.
