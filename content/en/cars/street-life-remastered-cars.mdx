## What the game confirms

The official description promises exotic cars, and Roblox's badge list has "Buy your first car!", "Buy your first boat!" and "Won a Rare Car!". The last one says you won a rare vehicle by spinning the wheel, so the wheel spin is a real way to get rare cars.

## Three cars, as one creator tested them

Kx's video "THE BEST CARS YOU NEED TO USE IN STREET LIFE REMASTERED 2!" (November 21, 2025) covers three cars he likes. The title says "2", and this site does not treat that as a different game.

| Car | What he said |
| --- | --- |
| Armored Karuma | Only from the wheel spin, which he put at about a 2% chance. Good speed but heavy to turn. He called it the best of the armored cars compared with the SWAT car |
| Huracan | Fast and smooth, drifts well, hood can be taken off and put back. It explodes quickly when shot. With premium he thinks you can change the flame color |
| G80 | His long-time daily car, used before he had any other |

The 2% chance and the premium flame color are his statements, not official figures.

## Cars for robberies

For house looting, Mix says to use a pickup truck such as a TRX or Silverado so you can carry furniture (August 2026). For fights, Kx says any car that gets shot can blow up, so keep moving.
