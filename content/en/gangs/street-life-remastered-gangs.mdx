## How gangs work

The official description says you can "join a faction and take over turfs for prizes". In practice, creators describe gangs as Roblox groups plus a Discord server, each tied to a turf on the map. A turf is a hideout with gun crates, and in the videos below it is the main reason to join a gang.

## How to join a gang

The videos describe joining their particular gangs. Membership and crate-access rules can differ between communities:

1. In Roblox, open **More**, then **Communities**.
2. Search for the gang's group name and join the group.
3. Read the group's current access instructions. If it requires a Discord role or ticket, use the link supplied by that group.
4. Back in game, walk to the gang's turf.

Mix's June 2026 video adds one step for his gang, Heaven Sent: open a ticket in its Discord to get free access to the turf gun crates. He put the group at about 78,000 members at that time. Ask each gang's Discord how access works, because it is set by the gang.

## How to find STB / Spin The Block

The Roblox community is **STB / Spin The Block**, group ID **15258900**. Its own description lists Street Life Remastered among the games where it has turfs. The community API was checked on October 3, 2026; that description is the group's claim, not a guarantee that every crate is open to every member.

1. Open the [STB Roblox community](https://www.roblox.com/communities/15258900) and check the name and ID.
2. Use Roblox's membership controls and read the community's current instructions.
3. Check whether turf or crate access requires an additional role or ticket; the public description alone does not establish this.
4. Return to the game and test the access you actually have. Joining a Roblox group and unlocking every turf feature are separate steps.

## Looking for the 762 gang?

Check the exact community identity in the game's official community or current in-game information before following a joining route.

## What a turf offers, as shown

- **Heaven Sent (Mix, June 2026):** a turf with a bar, an arcade and a basketball court, gun crates holding what he called every gun in the game, three paint options for weapons, and four gang outfits in a premium room. These are his claims about his own gang.
- **Revenge (DopegeekG, March 2025):** recommended as a beginner gang. Its crate room had an AK, a military vest, a Glock switch and a Tech 9, among others. He said it was the most joined gang at the time.
- **Sinister (DopegeekG, March 2025):** the creator had to pay money to join it.

## Get the game's own group too

Mix also tells viewers to join the game's official community on Roblox, which links to the official Discord server. An API snapshot read on September 19, 2026 showed about 1.0 million members; this is not a current member count.
