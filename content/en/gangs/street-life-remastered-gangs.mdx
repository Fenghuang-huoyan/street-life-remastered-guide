## Quick answer

Gangs in Street Life Remastered are usually a **Roblox community + Discord**, tied to a **turf** with gun crates and other hideout features. Joining the Roblox group is step one; crate access often needs an extra role or ticket. STB / Spin The Block (group **15258900**) is the community we verified by API; other gang names in old videos may be inactive.

## Executable steps: join a gang (generic)

1. In Roblox, open **More → Communities**.
2. Search the gang’s **exact group name** (verify the group ID when you can).
3. Join the group, then read the **current** wall / shout / Discord link—do not reuse a year-old YouTube comment.
4. If the gang requires a Discord role or ticket, open that ticket and wait for the role they assign.
5. Return to Street Life Remastered and walk to that gang’s **turf**.
6. Test crate / door access yourself. Group membership ≠ full turf unlock.

## Executable steps: STB / Spin The Block

Verified community: **[STB / Spin The Block](https://www.roblox.com/communities/15258900)** · group ID **15258900** · public entry allowed on the October 5, 2026 API check · description lists Street Life Remastered among games with turfs.

1. Open the community link above and confirm name + ID **15258900**.
2. Join with Roblox membership controls.
3. Read STB’s current instructions for Street Life Remastered (not for a different game on the same group).
4. Check whether turf or crate access needs **Family / Half / Full / Dragon / Diamond** (or another) role—see table below.
5. Ask STB staff in their current channel if the public page does not spell out promotion rules.
6. In-game, test only the access your role actually unlocks.
