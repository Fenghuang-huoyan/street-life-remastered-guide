## Where guns come from

- **Turf crates.** Gang turfs hold gun crates. Mix says his gang's crates hold what he described as every gun in the game (June 2026), and DopegeekG lists an AK, Glock switch and Tech 9 in the Revenge crates (March 2025).
- **Gun stores.** Any store with a water-gun sign works. You can buy ammo and guns there, but DopegeekG calls it a poor early option because it costs a lot.
- **Group rewards.** Mix's August 2026 run gave a Scorpion gun from the group rewards.

## One creator's top five

This is not our ranking. It is AqqleHQ's list from the video "Top 5 Best Guns In Street Life Remastered Roblox..." published June 3, 2025, with his stated reasons. Guns may have been rebalanced since.

| Rank | Gun | Reason given |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | ARP Dragon | Good damage, speed and reload animation. He describes it as exclusive to a turf, but the caption there is garbled, so which turf is unconfirmed |
| 2 | Honeybadger | He called it the most damage he had seen, with decent fire rate and reload |
| 3 | Glock Switch | Fully automatic, extended magazine with 30 rounds |
| 4 | Tech 9 | Cheap, automatic, fast reload, decent damage |
| 5 | M16 | Good damage, slow-ish fire rate. Not in the gun store; he found it at the Revenge turf |

## Unconfirmed

- Damage, fire rate and magazine numbers. Only the descriptions above were given, not stats.
- Whether the order still holds after the summer 2025 gun update and later changes.
- Event guns. A creator mentions a Christmas gun, but nothing here confirms its stats or return date.
