## How to read this page

Every number is what a creator showed or said in a specific video. Payouts change with updates and vary per run. On October 15, 2026 the Roblox games API reported an experience update that same day. Neither video below was recorded after that timestamp, so treat the figures as scale from the video date.

The main walkthrough is Mix's video "How to Make $2,000,000 in STREET LIFE REMASTERED (Roblox)" from August 16, 2026. He starts that run with no cash and reaches almost $2 million. He says he gave about $400,000 of it away, and that the cooldown on the museum, jewelry store, and bank, which he puts at roughly 10 to 30 minutes, is what made the run slow. A second video, Trizzleyy's "BEST MONEY METHODS IN: Street Life Remastered" from April 25, 2026, shows steps Mix skipped and a few figures that do not match August. Those mismatches are marked Unconfirmed below, not averaged.

The game has an official badge, "You got 1M!", for holding over $1 million in the bank. See [Badges](/achievements/street-life-remastered-badges).

## What to do first

This is the order Mix used on August 16, 2026, starting from $0 cash. It is his route, not a ranking of every method in the game.

**1. Claim the group rewards.** He called this the easiest cash and got about $12,000 plus a Scorpion. These are not redeem codes. See [Codes](/codes/street-life-remastered-codes) and [Guns](/guns/street-life-remastered-guns).

**2. Rob the nearby stores with a gun.** In that run the supermarket connected to the dealership paid about $7,000, and the gas station paid about $11,000. A corner store on the same street could not be robbed at that moment. He also broke into a car while he was there, but he did not say what the car paid. Two stores by the chop shop then paid about $15,000 each. He put the small robberies together at about $60,000. Other players drove up during the second chop-shop store. See [Robberies](/robberies/street-life-remastered-robberies).

**3. Buy printers before the slower loops.** He bought a basic printer for $2,000 that returns $4,000, then premium printers he put at about $15,000 every 5 minutes. He hid them behind the chop shop and told viewers to place printers somewhere safe. Collect on that timer while you do the next steps. An April video describes different printer timers. That disagreement is in the printer section.

**4. Buy supplies at the merchant.** Mix bought C4, duffel bags, credit cards, and a lot of lockpicks.

**5. Loot houses with a pickup.** He used a TRX or a Silverado, lockpicked the door, loaded furniture, and sold at the warehouse. Called-out prices from that run: one sale about $5,000, a fridge about $7,000, a safe about $15,000. After those sales his cash was about $100,000, which includes the earlier robberies. See [Cars](/cars/street-life-remastered-cars).

**6. Use the waits.** While big robberies cool down, he pointed at the yacht, oil, the wheel, playtime rewards, jobs, trash cans, zone captures, and crypto. The yacht and oil have figures below. The rest were named, not measured.

## Store robberies

**1.** Bring a gun. Mix pulls it on the clerk, and the clerk hands over the cash.

**2.** Spots that paid in the August 16 run: the supermarket connected to the dealership (about $7,000), the gas station (about $11,000 the first time and about $15,000 on a later pass), and two stores by the chop shop (about $15,000 each).

**3.** A later pass on smaller stores, after the houses, paid about $7,000 and then about $10,000. One gas station was not ready when he first returned.

**4.** Leave if other players arrive. In the video, people pulled up mid-robbery and cash was still sitting on the counter.

March 2025, DopegeekG showed a store at about $2,000. That is lower than every store Mix priced in August 2026. Mix says later updates added methods and rebalanced others, which fits the gap. Use the 2026 figures as the newer scale, and expect them to move again.

## Money printers

**1.** Buy them once the small robberies have paid. Mix did this before houses so the printers could run in the background.

**2.** In the August 16 video the basic printer costs $2,000 and returns $4,000. He then bought premium printers and said they give about $15,000 every 5 minutes.

**3.** Place them somewhere safe. He put his behind the chop shop.

**4.** Come back, collect, and place more if you can. On one pickup he counted "10, 20, 30 bands" and his cash was about $132,000, up from about $100,000 after the house sales.

**5.** Keep doing other methods during the 5 minutes. He described that as making money two ways at once.

Trizzleyy, on April 25, 2026, bought printers from a computer inside an apartment he claimed. He read one timer as 15 minutes for $30,000 and two more as 10 minutes for $20,000, and he added those to $50,000. He did not wait the timers out on camera. Those figures do not match Mix's basic and premium printers from August. **Unconfirmed** whether the April apartment stock and the August printers are the same products, or which timers are in the game now.

## Houses

**1.** Buy lockpicks from the merchant. Mix said to buy a lot of them.

**2.** Get a pickup. Mix named a TRX or a Silverado, because the truck has to carry furniture. Trizzleyy's April run used a Silverado only.

**3.** Lockpick the door.

**4.** Load what you can carry. Mix took a fridge, a microwave, a couch, and a table from the first place, then a TV and a safe from a second house.

**5.** Sell at the warehouse. Mix put that warehouse next to the kick store and gun shop number two. At the second house he sold to a buyer who was already next to the building.

| Item | What was shown |
| --- | --- |
| First sale in both videos | about $5,000 (Mix, August 16, 2026, and Trizzleyy, April 25, 2026) |
| Fridge | about $7,000 (Mix only) |
| Safe | about $15,000 (Mix only) |
| Second item in the April run | Trizzleyy estimated the two items together at about $10,000 to $11,000, and he was not precise |

After Mix sold that run's furniture, his cash was about $100,000. That number includes the store robberies from earlier in the video.

## Oil

Mix, August 16, 2026:

**1.** Pump the oil. He said there are four pumps now, up from three, and that the extra pump is more money. He did not say what a pump costs.

**2.** Refine it at the warehouse. He said the higher quality sells for more.

**3.** Sell the refined oil to the loot buyer. That sale was about $8,000, in the same stop as a diamond watch for about $25,000.

Trizzleyy, April 25, 2026, showed a different cost and was unsure about refining:

**1.** At the oil rig, go up the stairs and get the oil at the top.

**2.** He said it costs $5,000 a pump.

**3.** Wait for the pump to finish, then use the oil filling table. That gave him crude oil.

**4.** He was not sure refining raises the price. He refined it anyway and sold it to the loot buyer for $9,000. He did not say how many pumps that $9,000 covered.

**Unconfirmed:** the pump price after April 25, and whether a refined sale is per pump or per batch. Do not subtract $5,000 from $8,000. The two videos never show the cost and the sale for the same load.

## Farm grow (April 2026 only)

Mix named a plant job in August and never showed it paying. Trizzleyy, on April 25, 2026, called a farm grow one of the newer methods and walked through it:

**1.** On a keyboard, open the map with M. He put the farm next to supply and the ice cream spot.

**2.** Buy a pumpkin. He said the sunflower cannot be bought without Robux. He thought bone marrow also needs Robux, and he did not know the price.

**3.** Use garden soil, water, and a seed. He skipped instant grow.

**4.** Wait 15 minutes, then collect.

He guessed the pumpkin paid about $10,000 because his cash moved from about $48,000 to about $58,000, and he said he was not sure. **Unconfirmed** payout, and **Unconfirmed** whether this is the same activity as the plant job Mix named in August.

## Pro pad and the chop shop

Trizzleyy tried to steal a parked player car with a pro pad and sell it at the chop shop. He said the link takes about 30 seconds, that you can be killed during it, and that the other player loses cash. He died mid-link and never showed a payout. There is no measured income for this method in that video.
